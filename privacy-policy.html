B. CV Upload (Optional)

If you choose to upload your CV, the file is transmitted to our Google Apps Script backend where it is read and used to auto-fill the registration form. The extracted text is processed by the Gemini API to identify your skills and target roles. The raw file may be stored temporarily in a private Google Drive accessible only to the service operator (Brij Best) and is deleted within 30 days. You are not required to upload a CV — manual form completion is fully supported.

C. Chancenkarte Calculator

The Chancenkarte Points Calculator operates entirely within your browser using JavaScript. No data is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. Your answers exist only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session and are permanently deleted when you close or refresh the page.

D. Technical Logs

Our Google Apps Script backend generates operational logs for service reliability, including timestamps of email sends, API fetch summaries, and error records. These logs contain email addresses and are retained for 6 months for debugging purposes, after which they are deleted.

07 Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject under the GDPR (Articles 15–22), you have the following rights. To exercise any of them, email bestbrij1@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

📋 Right of Access (Art. 15)

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you, including what we process, why, and who we share it with.

✏️ Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

Request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data we hold about you.

🗑️ Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

"Right to be forgotten." Request deletion of your data. We will delete all your records within 30 days. One-click unsubscribe in every email also triggers deletion.

⏸️ Right to Restriction (Art. 18)

Request that we restrict processing of your data (e.g. pause emails while you travel) without deleting your profile.

📦 Right to Portability (Art. 20)

Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV/JSON). We will provide your registration data within 30 days.

🚫 Right to Object (Art. 21)

Object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also withdraw consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email.

You also have the right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Our Gemini AI matching generates email content suggestions only — all data storage, email delivery, and account management decisions are made by the human operator (Brij Best).

09 AI Processing — Google Gemini

We use the Google Gemini API to power the "AI Match Reason" feature in your daily job digest. Here is exactly what is sent to Gemini and what is not:

Data sent to Gemini:

  • Your target job titles and skills (e.g. "AML Analyst, KYC, Python")
  • Your visa status category (e.g. "Chancenkarte Holder")
  • Your German language level
  • Your city and work mode preferences
  • A list of anonymised job listing text (title, company, city, description excerpt)

Data NOT sent to Gemini:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Any other directly identifying information

Gemini returns a JSON array of job matches with a one-sentence match reason per job. This output is used to compose your email and is not stored beyond the email send event.

Under our API usage agreement, Google does not use data sent via the Gemini API to train its AI models. See Google's Gemini API Terms of Service.
