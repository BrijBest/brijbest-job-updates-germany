The Opportunity You May Not Know About

The Chancenkarte opens Germany's door. We help you walk through it.

Germany's Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) lets qualified non-EU nationals live in Germany for up to 12 months to find a full-time job — even without a job offer. Over 11,000 visas have been issued since June 2024, with India as the largest source country. If you qualify, this is the fastest legal route to employment in Germany.
