Nature of Service

BrijBest Job Updates Agent - Germany is a free job alert aggregation service for international professionals seeking employment in Germany. It aggregates publicly available job listings from third-party sources including the Bundesagentur für Arbeit (Federal Employment Agency of Germany), Arbeitnow.com, and Remotive.com.

BrijBest is not a recruitment agency and does not receive any commission, fee, or payment from employers for candidate placement or referral. Listings are provided for informational purposes only.
